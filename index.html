📋 What Is geo-mcp-servers?

geo-mcp-servers is a curated, tracked list of Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. Think of MCP servers as small helper programs that let different geospatial tools talk to each other. This repository gives you an easy way to find and use servers for:

  • 📍 Geocoding — convert addresses to coordinates and vice versa.
  • 🗺️ Routing — calculate directions and travel times.
  • 🗄️ PostGIS — work with spatial databases.
  • 🛰️ STAC & Imagery — access satellite and aerial imagery.
  • 🏗️ QGIS & ArcGIS — integrate with popular GIS software.
  • 🌦️ Weather — fetch real-time weather data.
  • 🏢 Commercial Location Platforms — use services from providers like Google Maps, Mapbox, and others.
